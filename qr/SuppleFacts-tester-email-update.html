Most of you can ignore this. It only applies if you added the SuppleFacts icon to your phone's home screen before today, October 10. If you didn't, there is nothing to do. Keep using the QR code or link I sent you, which now takes you to the current version with the barcode scanner.

If you did add the icon before today, please replace it. The icon you saved opens an older copy of the app that does not have the scanner, so please delete it and add it again:

  1. Delete the old icon. On an iPhone, press and hold it, choose Remove App, then Delete. On Android, press and hold it, then choose Uninstall or Remove.
  2. Open this address in your phone's browser (Safari on iPhone, Chrome on Android): https://supplefacts.brokenpromiseshealthcare.org
  3. Add it to your home screen again. On iPhone, tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen. On Android, tap the menu (three dots), then Install app or Add to Home screen.
  4. Open the app and tap "Scan a barcode." The first time, your phone will ask permission to use the camera. Please say yes.
